How these numbers work
Interest rate
Freddie Mac's Primary Mortgage Market Survey, published each Thursday. It is the national average for a 30 year fixed loan with 20% down and strong credit. Jumbo loans, FHA, VA and lower credit scores can price differently.
Principal and interest
A standard 30 year fixed payment on the price minus your down payment.
Mortgage insurance
With less than 20% down, most loans add mortgage insurance. I estimate it at 0.5% of the loan a year. Yours could be higher or lower.
Property tax and insurance
Tax starts at 1.25% of the price a year. Insurance starts at 0.35% of the price a year. Both are estimates you can change above.
Escrow and closing costs
I estimate them at 6% of the price when you use a loan and 3% when you pay cash. That covers lender fees, escrow, title, prepaid taxes and insurance, and similar costs. Your lender and escrow officer give you the real figures.
